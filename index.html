More information

What are we doing at Community Fitness Sarajevo (CFS) ?

Our training is not only fun but also one of the most efficient ways to achieve results, especially for those with busy schedules. High-intensity workouts combined with functional movements deliver progress faster than you might expect. We focus on constantly varied, functional movements performed at a level of intensity tailored to you. This method produces measurable outcomes, helping you improve strength, endurance, and overall fitness. Our dedicated coaches will guide you through the foundational movements and adapt each workout to suit your current fitness level. Whether you're just starting your fitness journey or looking to take it to the next level, we're here to support you every step of the way.

Do I need to be fit to start training at Community Fitness Sarajevo?

Not at all! You don’t need to be fit to join—we’re here to help you get there. While some movements might seem challenging at first, we’ll adjust the workouts to suit your current ability, ensuring your safety and steady progress.

What does a training session look like and how does it fit into only an hour?

People often hesitate to start a fitness regimen because they are unsure of what to do, how to warm up, perform movements correctly, and they don’t enjoy working out alone. Our dedicated Coaches guide you through a 60-minute class with the following components:

  • Warm-Up: The coach will take the class through a warm-up to prepare you for the rest of the session.
  • Strength or Skill Work: After the warm-up, we lead the class through a skill/strength practice, typically related to the movements in that day’s workout.
  • Workout: Once everyone is ready, we start the clock, and the entire class does the workout together.
  • Cooldown: Fist bumps, sharing scores, and stretching to transition you back into your day.

With this structure, each session is designed to maximize efficiency, ensure proper technique, and foster a supportive community environment.

What we offer at Community Fitness Sarajevo?

At Community Fitness Sarajevo, we provide a fitness experience like no other. Our group classes are kept small, with a maximum of 12-16 people, ensuring you receive the attention and guidance you deserve. Our coaches are not only certified and competent but also bring 9 years of CrossFit coaching experience to every session. They are dedicated to making your workouts both fun and challenging.


We believe in the power of a great community. When you join us, you're not just signing up for a workout; you're becoming part of a supportive and motivating community that will cheer you on every step of the way. Come and experience the difference at Community Fitness Sarajevo, where fitness meets community.

What should I expect at my first class?

Feeling a bit nervous is completely normal! Don’t worry—we’ll guide you through everything, from what to bring and where to find your coach, to learning the basics of our training style. Our goal is to make you feel comfortable, confident, and excited to start your journey.
